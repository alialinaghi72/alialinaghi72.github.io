Grade unit conversions

Base-metal grades are usually given in percent and precious-metal grades in grams per tonne or ounces per ton. The key relationships:

UnitEquivalent
1 %10,000 ppm = 10,000 g/t
1 ppm1 g/t = 1,000 ppb
1 troy oz per short ton (oz/st)≈ 34.286 g/t
1 troy ounce31.1035 g
1 metric tonne2,204.62 lb

Tonnage and contained metal

Tonnage (t) = Volume (m³) × Bulk density (t/m³)
Contained metal = Tonnage × Grade

For a percent grade, contained metal in tonnes is tonnage times grade divided by 100; for a g/t grade, tonnage times grade gives contained metal in grams. Contained metal is an in-situ quantity and excludes mining and processing recovery.

Why density matters

An error in density becomes an error in tonnage and contained metal directly. Density should be set from enough representative measurements for each domain and rock type — especially in iron deposits, where density changes with grade, and in more porous oxide zones.

Questions

How many grams per tonne is one percent?

One percent equals 10,000 g/t, or 10,000 ppm.

How do I convert ounces per ton to grams per tonne?

One troy ounce per US short ton is about 34.286 g/t, and one troy ounce per metric tonne is about 31.103 g/t.
