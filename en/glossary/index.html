Exploration and resource estimation glossary

Persian equivalents and short definitions of the terms used most in exploration and resource-estimation reports.

81 terms

A

Alterationدگرسانی
Changes in host-rock mineralogy and chemistry caused by hydrothermal fluids, such as potassic, phyllic, argillic and propylitic alteration.
Anisotropyناهمسانگردی
Directional differences in grade continuity, described by an anisotropy ellipsoid.
Anomalyآنومالی
A value significantly different from the regional background that may indicate mineralisation. More →
ASTERسنجندهٔ ASTER
A multispectral satellite sensor with short-wave and thermal infrared bands, widely used for alteration mapping. More →

B

Band ratioنسبت باندی
Dividing two spectral bands of a satellite image to highlight minerals or alteration zones. More →
Blankنمونهٔ شاهد
Material without the element of interest, used to reveal contamination during preparation and assay.
Block modelمدل بلوکی
A 3D representation of a deposit as regular blocks, each carrying grade, density, domain code and classification. More →
Bulk densityچگالی توده‌ای
The mass per unit volume of in-situ rock, including voids; essential for converting volume to tonnage. More →

C

Carlin-type goldطلای نوع کارلین
Sediment-hosted gold carried as invisible gold in arsenian pyrite, with an arsenic–antimony–mercury association.
Certified reference material (CRM)نمونهٔ استاندارد
Material with a certified grade and known standard deviation, used to measure laboratory accuracy. More →
Change of supportتغییر تکیه‌گاه
Converting a grade distribution from sample or large-block scale to SMU scale, for example by uniform conditioning.
Collarدهانهٔ گمانه
The starting point of a drillhole at surface, with its coordinates and elevation.
Competent Personشخص صلاحیت‌دار
A member of a recognised professional body with at least five years of relevant experience who takes responsibility for a public report under JORC.
Compositingترکیب‌سازی
Regularising sample lengths before statistics and estimation so that every datum carries equal volume weight.
Core recoveryبازیابی مغزه
The ratio of recovered core length to drilled length; an indicator of sample quality.
Cut-off gradeعیار حد
The lowest grade at which sending material to a given destination, such as the mill, is economic. More →

D

Declusteringوزن‌زدایی خوشه‌ها
Correcting for preferential, dense sampling — usually of high-grade areas — to obtain a representative mean and distribution.
Detailed explorationاکتشاف تفصیلی
The stage of systematic drilling and sampling for resource estimation and feasibility studies. More →
Detection limitحد تشخیص
The lowest concentration an analytical method can reliably distinguish from zero.
Diamond (core) drillingحفاری مغزه‌ای
Drilling with a diamond bit that produces continuous rock core for geological study and sampling.
Dilutionرقیق‌شدگی
Waste or low-grade material mixed with ore during mining, lowering the feed grade.
Domainingدامنه‌بندی
Dividing a deposit into geologically and statistically homogeneous zones that are estimated separately. More →
Downhole surveyانحراف‌سنجی گمانه
Measuring a drillhole's azimuth and dip at intervals to define its true path.
Duplicateنمونهٔ تکراری
A second split of a sample at one preparation stage, used to measure precision.

E

Epithermal depositکانسار اپی‌ترمال
A gold–silver deposit formed at shallow depth and relatively low temperature in volcanic settings.

F

Feasibility study (FS)مطالعهٔ امکان‌سنجی
The most comprehensive techno-economic study, used as the basis for investment and financing decisions.

G

General explorationاکتشاف عمومی
The stage of trenching, ground geophysics and first drillholes to confirm mineralisation. More →
Gossanگوسان
An iron-oxide cap formed by weathering of sulphides at surface; it can indicate mineralisation below.
Grade–tonnage curveمنحنی عیار‑تناژ
A plot of tonnage and average grade above cut-off for a range of cut-off grades. More →

H

Half absolute relative difference (HARD)شاخص HARD
Half the absolute difference between two duplicate results divided by their mean; a precision measure.

I

Indicated Resourceمنبع نشان‌داده‌شده
A resource category with enough confidence for mine planning and economic evaluation.
Indicator krigingکریجینگ شاخص
Kriging of 0/1 indicators at several grade thresholds to estimate the probability of exceeding each one; suited to highly skewed, high-nugget distributions. More →
Induced polarization (IP)قطبش القایی (IP)
A geophysical method that measures the ground's ability to store electrical charge temporarily; used to detect disseminated sulphides. More →
Inferred Resourceمنبع استنباطی
The lowest-confidence resource category, with continuity implied but not verified; it does not convert to reserves.
Inverse distance weighting (IDW)وزن‌دهی عکس فاصله
Estimation by a weighted average in which each sample's weight is proportional to an inverse power of its distance.
Iron oxide–apatite (Kiruna-type) depositکانسار اکسید آهن‑آپاتیت
A magnetite–apatite deposit related to magmatic activity, also called Kiruna-type, such as the iron deposits of the Bafq district.

J

JORC Codeکد JORC
The Australasian code for reporting Exploration Results, Mineral Resources and Ore Reserves; a member of the CRIRSCO family. More →

K

Krigingکریجینگ
A geostatistical estimation method that estimates the grade of a point or block as a weighted average of samples, with weights derived from the variogram model so that estimation error variance is minimised. More →
Kriging neighbourhood analysis (KNA)تحلیل همسایگی کریجینگ
Testing search-neighbourhood parameters against metrics such as slope of regression and kriging efficiency before the final estimate.
Kriging varianceواریانس کریجینگ
The estimation error variance of kriging; it depends only on sample geometry and the variogram model, not on grade values.

L

Lagگام فاصله
The distance class used to group sample pairs when calculating an experimental variogram.

M

Magnetometryمغناطیس‌سنجی
Measuring variations in the Earth's magnetic field to trace magnetic minerals, intrusions and structures. More →
Measured Resourceمنبع اندازه‌گیری‌شده
The resource category with the highest geological confidence, in which geological and grade continuity are confirmed.
Metal equivalentعیار معادل
Expressing the value of several metals as the grade of one reference metal, accounting for each metal's price and recovery.
Metallurgical recoveryبازیابی متالورژیکی
The percentage of metal in the feed that reports to the product during processing.
Mineral Resourceمنبع معدنی
A concentration or occurrence of material of economic interest whose form, quality and quantity give reasonable prospects for eventual economic extraction. More →
Mining recoveryبازیابی استخراج
The percentage of in-situ ore that is actually mined and delivered.
Mississippi Valley-type (MVT) depositکانسار نوع دره می‌سی‌سی‌پی
A carbonate-hosted lead–zinc deposit formed from low-temperature basinal brines.
Modifying Factorsعوامل تعدیل‌کننده
The mining, processing, metallurgical, infrastructure, economic, marketing, legal, environmental, social and governmental considerations that convert a resource into a reserve.

N

Nearest neighbour (NN)نزدیک‌ترین همسایه
Assigning each block the grade of its closest sample; used for bias checks and declustering.
Net smelter return (NSR)ارزش خالص ذوب
Net revenue per tonne of ore after smelting, refining, freight and penalties; a common measure in polymetallic deposits.
NI 43-101NI 43‑101
The Canadian standard for disclosure on mineral projects, which uses the CIM definitions.
Nugget effectاثر قطعه‌ای
The variogram value at near-zero distance; it combines very short-scale variability with sampling, preparation and assay errors. More →

O

Ordinary krigingکریجینگ معمولی
The most common form of kriging; it assumes an unknown local mean that is constant within the neighbourhood, and its weights sum to one. More →
Ore Reserveذخیرهٔ کانسنگ
The economically mineable part of a Measured or Indicated Resource after applying the Modifying Factors. More →
Orogenic goldطلای کوهزایی
Vein-hosted gold related to shear zones and metamorphism in orogenic belts.

P

Pathfinder elementعنصر ردیاب
An element associated with mineralisation that forms a broader halo, such as arsenic for gold. More →
Payabilityدرصد قابل پرداخت
The share of contained metal in a concentrate that the buyer or smelter pays for.
Porphyry copper depositکانسار مس پورفیری
A large-tonnage, low-grade copper deposit, with molybdenum or gold, associated with porphyritic intrusions in magmatic arcs. More →
Pre-feasibility study (PFS)مطالعهٔ پیش‌امکان‌سنجی
A techno-economic study detailed enough to select mining and processing methods; the minimum needed to declare a reserve.
Probable Ore Reserveذخیرهٔ احتمالی
The economic part of an Indicated Resource, or of a Measured Resource whose Modifying Factors carry uncertainty.
Prospectingپی‌جویی
The stage of mapping and geochemical sampling to define anomalies and targets. More →
Proved Ore Reserveذخیرهٔ قطعی
The economic part of a Measured Resource, with the highest level of confidence.

Q

QA/QCکنترل کیفیت (QA/QC)
Quality assurance (preventing errors) and quality control (measuring errors with control samples) for exploration data. More →

R

Rangeدامنهٔ واریوگرام
The distance beyond which samples have no meaningful spatial correlation.
Reasonable prospects for eventual economic extraction (RPEEE)چشم‌انداز معقول استخراج اقتصادی
The condition any material must meet to be reported as a Mineral Resource under CRIRSCO-family codes.
Reconnaissanceشناسایی
The first exploration stage: reviewing regional data and making first visits to select promising areas. More →
Reverse circulation (RC) drillingحفاری چرخشی معکوس
Drilling that returns rock chips to surface through an inner tube; faster and cheaper than core drilling.

S

Scoping studyمطالعهٔ مقدماتی
A preliminary, low-accuracy techno-economic assessment that may rely on Inferred Resources.
SEDEX depositکانسار سدکس
A sedimentary-exhalative lead–zinc deposit formed in extensional sedimentary basins.
Selective mining unit (SMU)واحد انتخاب معدنی
The smallest volume that can practically be mined separately as ore or waste.
Sequential Gaussian simulation (SGS)شبیه‌سازی گاوسی متوالی
A conditional simulation method that generates equally probable realisations of the grade field, used to assess uncertainty.
Sillسقف واریوگرام
The level the variogram reaches beyond the range; approximately equal to the data variance.
Skarnاسکارن
A deposit formed where magmatic fluids react with carbonate rocks next to an intrusion.
Slope of regressionشیب رگرسیون
The slope of the regression of true block grade on estimated grade; values close to one indicate little conditional bias.
Supergene enrichmentغنی‌شدگی سوپرژن
Re-concentration of metal, especially copper, below the oxide zone by weathering and descending surface waters.
Swath plotنمودار Swath
A comparison of mean sample and block-model grades in parallel slices, used to validate an estimate.

T

Top-cut (capping)سقف‌گذاری عیار
Limiting extreme grades to a set value to control their disproportionate influence on the estimate.

U

Umpire assayآنالیز داور
Re-assay of a share of pulps at a second laboratory to reveal inter-laboratory bias.
UNFCطبقه‌بندی UNFC
The United Nations framework classifying resources and reserves on economic, feasibility and geological axes; the source of the three-digit codes common in Iranian reports. More →

V

Variogramواریوگرام
A function giving half the average squared difference between samples as a function of distance and direction; it describes the spatial continuity of grade. More →
